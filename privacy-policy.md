LoadMitra – Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 31, 2026

LoadMitra ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the LoadMitra mobile application collects, uses, stores, and shares information when you use our services.

By using LoadMitra, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use the app, we may collect the following information:

Account Information

- Name and display name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Account and authentication information
- Google sign-in information when you choose Google login

Identity Verification (KYC)

- Full name and address
- Citizenship or other identity document type and number
- Identity document photographs
- Driving licence details
- Vehicle registration and bluebook details
- Vehicle insurance information
- Company or business registration details, where applicable

KYC documents are stored in a private storage bucket and are used for identity and vehicle verification.

Profile Information

- Profile details
- Profile photograph
- Account status and user role

Profile photographs are stored in a publicly accessible storage bucket. Anyone who obtains a valid public image URL may be able to view the photograph.

Location Information

With your permission, LoadMitra may collect:

- GPS latitude and longitude
- Vehicle type and availability status
- Vehicle location update timestamps

Location information is used to display available vehicles and support transport-related services. When vehicle location sharing is started, location updates may be stored for up to one hour under the current expiry setting. You can stop sharing through the app.

Transport and Booking Information

- Pickup and delivery locations
- Goods type, weight and other shipment details
- Proposed prices and actual fares
- Booking details and status
- Trip distance and completion information
- Cancellation details and reasons

Messages and Support Information

- Messages exchanged between customers and transport providers
- Support requests, complaints and their categories
- Messages exchanged with the support team
- Notification details and timestamps

Payment-Related Records

The app may store payment-related records such as amount, currency, payment method, transaction reference and status, along with dispute information. This does not mean that LoadMitra currently processes payments through an integrated payment gateway.

Device and Technical Information

The app may process technical information needed for authentication, app operation, location services and notification registration.

2. How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

- Create and manage user accounts
- Verify user identity, vehicles and businesses
- Connect customers with transport providers
- Display available vehicles and location information
- Manage bookings, transport requests and trip history
- Enable communication between users
- Provide customer support and resolve complaints
- Send account verification emails and service notifications
- Detect misuse, protect the platform and maintain security
- Comply with applicable legal obligations

3. How Information Is Stored and Shared

LoadMitra uses Supabase for authentication, database services and file storage.

Information may be processed by service providers that help us operate the app, including:

- Supabase: Authentication, database and file storage.
- Brevo: Delivery of account verification emails.
- Google: Authentication when you choose Google sign-in.
- OpenFreeMap: Map display and map tile services.
- Facebook: External support links, if you choose to contact us through Facebook or Messenger.

We do not sell your personal information.

Information may be shared with relevant customers, transport providers or administrators when necessary to provide the requested services, verify accounts, manage bookings or resolve disputes.

We may also disclose information where required by law or to protect users and the platform.

4. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as necessary to operate the service, maintain account records, resolve disputes, meet legal obligations and protect the platform.

Some booking and transaction records may be retained after account deletion.

When an account deletion request is processed:

- Profile and KYC database records are removed as handled by the account deletion process.
- Associated KYC files and profile photographs are removed when the deletion process succeeds.
- The account is disabled.
- Email addresses and phone numbers may be retained in a restricted archive for account-deletion records.
- Transaction and booking history may be retained.

Therefore, account deletion does not necessarily mean that every record is immediately erased.

5. Your Choices and Rights

You can:

- Update permitted profile information.
- Control location permission through your device settings.
- Stop vehicle location sharing through the app.
- Request account deletion through the account deletion option in the app.
- Contact us to ask about your personal information or raise a privacy concern.

Some information may be retained where necessary for legal, security, dispute-resolution or transaction-record purposes.

6. Data Security

We use access controls and security measures intended to protect personal information.

KYC documents are stored in a private storage bucket. Profile photographs are stored separately in a public bucket.

However, no internet-based service or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Third-Party Services

Some features rely on third-party services. Their handling of information is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

Please review the relevant third-party policies when using Google, Supabase, Brevo, OpenFreeMap or Facebook services.

8. Children's Privacy

LoadMitra is not intended for children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 18.

If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or legal requirements change. Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
